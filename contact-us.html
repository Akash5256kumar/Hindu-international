Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about contacting us.

1. How long does it take to get a response?

We aim to respond to enquiries as soon as possible. Response time may vary depending on the nature of your message. Please make sure you provide the correct email address so we can get back to you.

2. What should I include in my message?

Please include your name, a valid email address and a clear description of your question or enquiry. If your message is about a specific service, event or initiative, mention it so we can assist you more effectively.

3. Is this email for general enquiries only?

You can contact us at service@hindu.international for general enquiries, support, feedback and questions related to our activities. Please explain your query clearly so we can direct it appropriately.
