Our Purpose

We aim to help devotees who cannot personally attend the sacred event. Distance, age, health, work, family responsibilities or other personal reasons can keep a devotee away from the holy waters — but they should not keep anyone away from its blessings.

Through our service, we perform the holy ritual on your behalf using your photograph, and share the recorded video with you, so that you can still receive the divine blessings and be a part of this sacred moment.
