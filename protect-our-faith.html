02 · Understanding the Issue

What Is Anti-Conversion?

Anti-conversion efforts seek to prevent or address religious conversion through force, coercion, fraud, deception, or unlawful inducement. The focus should be on protecting people from exploitation—not preventing anyone from freely choosing or changing their religion.

Why does it matter?

When religious decisions are influenced by threats, misleading promises, or exploitation of vulnerability, genuine freedom of conscience can be undermined. This can also cause emotional distress, family conflict, and concerns about cultural continuity.

03 · Understanding Vulnerability

Why Might People Become Vulnerable to Conversion Pressure?

These situations do not mean that every person who converts is pressured. They are circumstances in which exploitation may occur and where appropriate support can make a difference.

Financial hardship

Families facing poverty or debt may be especially vulnerable to promises of money or material assistance tied to religious expectations.

Education and healthcare needs

People seeking schooling, medical care, food, or essential assistance may be told that help is conditional on changing their beliefs.

Emotional distress and social isolation

During personal crises, loneliness, or family difficulties, people may seek acceptance, security, and a sense of belonging.

Deception, coercion, or misinformation

Threats, fraudulent claims, or misleading promises can undermine someone’s ability to make a genuinely informed decision.

04 · Our Response

How Can We Address Coercive Conversion?

Education and awareness

Teach Hindu philosophy, traditions, cultural heritage, and religious rights.

Healthcare and essentials

Help families access healthcare, food, and essential services without religious conditions.

Education and livelihoods

Expand learning opportunities, practical skills, and financial independence.

Legal guidance

Help people facing credible coercion or fraud understand their rights and seek appropriate assistance.

06 · Freedom, Dignity and Respect

Protect Our Faith. Respect Every Person.

We believe Hindu heritage deserves to be understood, preserved, and respected. We encourage people to learn about Hinduism, understand their roots, and make informed choices without fear or pressure.

If a Hindu freely chooses to follow another religion, that personal choice deserves respect. We also ask everyone to discuss Hinduism and Hindu communities honestly and respectfully, without spreading false claims, insults, or hatred. Differences in belief should never become a reason to demean a community.
